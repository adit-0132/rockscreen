Rockscreen turns your lock screen into a lyrics screen. Play a song, wake your phone, and the words are already there, scrolling in time with the music.

• Works with music apps that show playback controls in your notifications, such as Spotify
• Line-by-line synced lyrics from LRCLIB, the open, community-run lyrics database
• Album-art colours, play/pause/skip controls, and a choice of font, size and focus effect
• Keeps the screen on while lyrics play over the lock screen; press power to turn it off
• Closes as soon as you unlock

Private by design
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers
• Only the song title and artist leave your phone, sent to lrclib.net to look up lyrics
• Lyrics are cached on your phone for 30 days

Permissions
• Notification access: to see what is playing (title, artist, album, position)
• Notifications and full-screen notifications: to open the lyrics over the lock screen
• Display over other apps (optional): a fallback for phones that block full-screen notifications

Good to know
• You need a lock screen (swipe, PIN, pattern or fingerprint). With "None", Rockscreen has nowhere to appear
• If your phone unlocks the moment you wake it (for example a fingerprint sensor in the power button), wake it another way to see the lyrics, or leave it on a desk
• Lyrics exist only for songs in LRCLIB; remixes and very new releases are often missing
• Known issue: if a lookup fails (offline, LRCLIB down), that song shows no lyrics for 30 days
